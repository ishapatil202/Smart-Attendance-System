    What happens when you enroll?
    1. Your browser takes 3 snapshots and sends them to the Flask API.
    2. OpenCV finds your face and FaceNet turns each crop into a 128-d vector.
    3. Only the vectors are saved to SQLite. The images are discarded.
    4. Recognition compares a new frame's vector with every stored one (cosine similarity).
